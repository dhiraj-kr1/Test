About the game

A sandbox, not a level to beat

Wobble Shape drops colorful, squishy shapes onto a canvas and lets real physics take it from there. Flick a shape across the screen and it bounces, spins and settles exactly like a soft, springy toy would.

  • Soft-body wobble, bounce & drag physics
  • Type any word - each letter becomes a shape
  • Flip gravity, auto-drop, or toss everything at once
  • 14 color themes across light & dark mode
Features

Everything on the toolbar

A small set of controls that add up to a lot of ways to play.

Real physics

Gravity, bounce and collision tuned to feel soft and springy, not rigid.

Gravity flip

Flip to zero-g with one tap and watch every shape drift and float.

Auto drop

Sit back and let a steady rain of shapes fill the canvas on its own.

Toss all

Fling everything on screen at once for instant, satisfying chaos.

Type to spawn

Type any word or phrase - each letter becomes its own wobbly shape.

Dedicated shape modes

Switch to Circle, Triangle, Pentagon, Hexagon or Blob - every spawn matches.

Sound & feel

Satisfying pop and toss sound effects, or mute the whole canvas.

Light & dark

14 color palettes across a clean light mode and a true dark mode.

Themes

Every theme recolors the whole canvas - shapes, background and all.
